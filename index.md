# ToBeVPN

ToBeVPN is a VPN service. It encrypts your internet connection and routes it through ToBeVPN servers. We do not log visited sites, DNS queries or traffic content.

**Operator:** Individual Entrepreneur Ivan Zaichenko, Republic of Kazakhstan

[Русская версия ниже](#ru)

## Apps

- **Android and Android TV** — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tobevpn.app)
- **Windows 10/11** — Microsoft Store app built on the Windows VPN platform, no drivers or administrator rights required

## Features

- VLESS + REALITY encrypted transport
- Server list with live ping, automatic selection of the fastest server
- Per-app and per-site routing rules
- Traffic statistics and built-in speed test
- Sign-in with a Telegram account, no passwords; one subscription for all your devices
