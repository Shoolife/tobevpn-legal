## FAQ

**How do I sign in?**
Open the app and sign in with your Telegram account: scan the QR code with your phone or press "Sign in to own account". No password is needed.

**How do I connect?**
After signing in, press the large power button on the main screen. The status changes to "Connected".

**The Windows app is not visible after I close the window.**
Closing the window hides ToBeVPN to the notification area (tray). Click the tray icon to open it again, or choose "Exit" in its menu to quit.

**Does the Windows app install drivers?**
No. It uses the VPN platform built into Windows and appears in Settings → Network & internet → VPN as the "ToBeVPN" profile.

**How do I delete my account?**
See [Delete account](delete-account-en.html).
